Setup log (one-time cleanup, 11 Aug 2026 — not updated by later refreshes)

What was changed when this dashboard was first built from the sheet. Counts and current flags now live in the summary line above the table and the Flags KPI, since those need to stay accurate as the data changes — this log is historical.
  • Standardized every Status value from this sheet's raw free text (e.g. "Reestablishing relationship," "KYB in review," "conversion," "Viban in progress") onto the same fixed vocabulary as before — Status is still a text field in Asana, so this was a value-by-value rewrite, not a schema change.
  • Deleted a duplicate "Earth and Minerals" row and merged its Needs (Blaaiz Business + OTC) into the remaining one.
  • Moved "Digitech" from Pipeline to the Onboarding section (it was marked "Onboarded" but left in Pipeline) and archived it as Transacting.
  • Reactivation is a flag, not a stage — dormant clients coming back show at their real stage (usually Live) with a "Reactivating" tag, and their Asana Status reads "Live (Reactivating)".
  • Archived clients are marked complete in Asana with a Status ending in "(Archived)" instead of being deleted from the sheet, so churn/conversion history is kept — they're excluded from the live KPIs and track panels, and only show in the table with "Include archived" ticked.
  • Where Status was empty or too generic to place ("Onboarding" with no detail), the stage defaults to Compliance Review (onboarding) or Conversation (pipeline) and the account is flagged in the table and the Flags KPI for a human check — this rule re-applies automatically on every refresh, not just at setup.
  • Where Needs was empty, track defaults to Business and the account is marked with an asterisk in the table — also re-applied automatically each refresh.
